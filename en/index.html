I’m Tasmon. My story began in Brahmanbaria, wandered through Sylhet and Portsmouth, and brought me back to Sylhet.

Business, people and communication run through my studies, my work and my curiosity. Here’s the journey, told the way it happened rather than neatly tidied up.

Prologue Brahmanbaria Until September 2015

Where it began

I was born and grew up in Brahmanbaria. I’m the youngest in the family, with one elder brother and two sisters, and my father is a retired government officer.

School began at Brahmanbaria Mission Primary School at Mission House, founded in 1910. After that came SSC at Annada Government High School and HSC at Brahmanbaria Government College.

In September 2015, I set off for Sylhet to study further.

Chapter 1 Sylhet 2015 – 2020

First steps on campus

In Sylhet I enrolled at Army IBA, part of Bangladesh University of Professionals, and studied a BBA in Marketing and MIS. I graduated in 2020.

I stayed busy outside class too. I was an executive member of the Business Club and the Debate Club, and worked as Campus Coordinator for SERAC Bangladesh. Making an argument, leading a team, persuading someone: my first lessons in all of it came here.

And my volunteering with Volunteer for Bangladesh goes back many years.

Chapter 2 Sylhet 2020 – 2021

Learning on the job

In early 2020 I interned at Fiza & Co. I had no fixed position at first: I started in the corporate department, then moved to marketing. Fitting into a new place without a title was my first real-world lesson.

Then, from February to August 2021, I was a Trainee Executive Officer at Staff Asia. I took ownership of customer queries, flagged the urgent ones first, and worked with other departments to get things resolved. My pull towards customer service began there.

Around the same time I finished my MBA in Marketing at Metropolitan University, Sylhet (2021).

My thesis defence, which has to be presented in person, is still pending. Moving to the UK put it on hold. Finishing it is still on my list.

Chapter 3 Portsmouth, UK 2022 – 2024

Across the sea

In 2022 I won the Vice Chancellor’s Scholarship and flew to England. The University of Portsmouth awarded it to cover my tuition fees.

In 2023 I finished my MA there, in Business Communication for International Leadership. For my thesis I looked at how contactless payments affect consumer behaviour in the UK grocery market, under the guidance of Dr Alun Epps.

Earlier I had written another paper asking whether new media really is the superior choice for advertising in the digital age. Both are on ResearchGate.

In the middle of all this, on 8 December 2023, another big chapter began: I married Sadia Rahman Riya, who is a doctor.

In 2024 one more qualification joined the bag: the Chartered Management Institute (CMI) Level 7 Diploma in Strategic Management and Leadership Practice.

Chapter 4 HM Naval Base, Portsmouth May 2022 – June 2025

At the Navy museum

Alongside my studies I spent more than three years at the National Museum of the Royal Navy, as part of the Visitor Experience Team. One place, many roles:

  • Museum guideTelling the history, talking with visitors, and being responsible for fire evacuation and health and safety.
  • RetailKeeping stock available, displaying it well, and promoting sales.
  • AdmissionsSelling high-value entry tickets, explaining upgrades, and giving visitors the information they needed.
  • Children’s play areaRunning activities such as the climbing wall, Laser Quest and Ocean Warrior.
  • Private eventsOn-site help on the day.

Mixing easily with strangers, staying calm under pressure, and taking safety seriously: that job taught me all three.

Chapter 5 Sylhet November 2025 – August 2026

Back in Sylhet, between two countries

Back home, I started at TAJ Consulting Firm Ltd., a UK-based chartered accountancy firm, as Admin Officer in its Sylhet office.

A big part of the job was being the bridge between the Bangladesh and UK offices: handling client correspondence and documents, keeping records, managing schedules, preparing reports, watching compliance, and supporting senior management with accounting-related admin. Knowing how work is done in both countries made crossing that bridge easier.

Chapter 6 Sylhet September 2026 – present

Now, and the story goes on

I’m now Client Success Manager at Tent Communications. I look after client relationships, match our services to what clients are trying to achieve, and tie different departments together to raise client satisfaction.

Customer service, administration, strategy, research: everything I’ve learned so far has landed in one chair. But the story isn’t finished. I’m ready for the next chapter.
